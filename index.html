Good to know.

Can I buy insurance through this app?

This is an interactive Blueberry Insurance concept. You can explore the experience and create a sample estimate, but it doesn't issue policies or provide actual insurance quotes.

How does the sample estimate work?

The demo uses a simple illustrative formula based on vehicle type, annual mileage, and your chosen coverage level. It isn't underwriting and isn't a real rate.

What information do I need to try it?

Just a ZIP code, vehicle type, mileage, and a coverage preference. No name, payment details, or driver's license is needed. Entries stay in this browser session.

What’s the difference between comprehensive and collision?

Collision generally addresses damage from a crash. Comprehensive generally addresses other covered events, such as theft, weather, or fire. Actual terms depend on the policy.
